I.

No. 01 — The idea

There was a moment, around the turn of the millennium, when the mid-engine car was still mechanical. A gated manual. A naturally aspirated V8 that asked for nine thousand revolutions and meant every one of them. Then the screens arrived, and the paddles, and the turbos, and something went quiet.

Licori Automobili exists to pick up where that moment left off. We take one of the last analogue V8s to leave Modena and rebuild it, by hand and in small numbers, as the car its engineers might have built with today’s materials and none of the compromises.

We don’t emulate icons. We evolve them.

II.

No. 02 — Heart

Three point nine.

The name of our first car is a number. The flat-plane V8 at its centre grows from 3.6 to 3.95 litres, still breathing without a turbocharger, still built to sing past nine thousand rpm. More than five hundred horsepower, delivered the old way: through your right foot and a metal gate.

III.

No. 03 — Skin

The same line, in a lighter hand.

Every body panel is remade in carbon fibre, then painted, so you’ll rarely see it. You’ll feel it instead, in the way the car turns in and settles.

IV.

No. 04 — Atelier

Made by specialists, not suppliers.

The methods come from Formula 1 and aerospace: pre-preg carbon, autoclave-cured, held to tolerances measured in tenths of a millimetre. What makes it ours is attention. A craftsman’s eye on every ply, every seam and every fastener, before each car is assembled, tested and signed off in California.

V.

No. 05 — Interior

Cognac, carbon, cashmere.

Heritage leather that will age the way a good saddle does. Exposed carbon where the hand never rests. Wool and cashmere cloth from Italy’s finest mills where it does. Every cabin is specified with its owner, never chosen from a list.

VI.

No. 06 — Purpose

Meant for canyon roads, not climate-controlled garages.

Street-first, capable on track, and serviced like a road car, so it gets driven.

VII.

No. 07 — Edition

Fifty cars. Each one already spoken for before work begins.
